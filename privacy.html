Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This website and the “My Story” app belong to Bahar Uddin Raihan. They present my story, education, work and contact details. This policy explains what information is handled.

What I collect

Nothing. The site and app have no accounts, no forms, no analytics and no advertising. I do not collect, store or sell any personal data about visitors or app users.

Third-party services

  • YouTube. Videos are shown with a preview picture loaded from YouTube, so YouTube may receive your IP address when the page loads. When you press play, the video is embedded from YouTube (in privacy-enhanced mode) and YouTube may set its own cookies. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts. The text font is loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address.
  • Links. Links to news sites, Facebook, YouTube and any payment method listed under “Support” lead to services that have their own privacy policies.

Support

Support is optional. Any payment is made directly through the payment service you choose (for example bKash, Nagad or PayPal). I do not process payments or see your payment details on this site.

Data stored on your device

The app saves copies of the site's files on your device so it can open offline. This contains no personal information about you.

Content is shared for inspiration and encouragement only. It is not medical advice and not a substitute for professional care. If you are in crisis or in danger, please contact your local emergency services or a qualified professional.

Children
